Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

Alternaria solani • Foliar Fungal Complex
Moderate to High Alert
Model Confidence 94.8%

Fungal foliar disease characterized by dark brown concentric lesions ("bullseye pattern") starting on the oldest lower foliage. Elevated humidity and 27°C temperature have accelerated spore germination.

Pathological Foliar Symptoms

  • Concentric brown rings encircled by a chlorotic yellow halo on lower leaves
  • Basal leaf yellowing with progressive upward defoliation

Recommended Farm Treatment

  • Organic Remedy: Spray cold-pressed Neem Seed Kernel Extract (5%) or Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5g/L early in the morning.
  • Cultural Practice: Prune infected bottom foliage up to 30cm high. Mulch with straw to arrest fungal splashback.
  • Chemical Option (If severe): Apply Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L.
